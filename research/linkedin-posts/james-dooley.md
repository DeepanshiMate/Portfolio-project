## Post 1
**Topic:** You're Not Losing Rankings — You're Losing Clicks

AI Overviews now take a huge share of traffic even when you rank #1. The problem isn't being outranked — it's being skipped.

5 ways to adapt:

1. Write for extraction
   - Every section answers a question instantly
   - No fluff, no buildup — just clear standalone answers

2. Increase signal, not length
   - Original data, stats, and comparisons
   - Generic content gets ignored by AI

3. Structure everything
   - Schema, FAQs, tables
   - Make it easy for machines to understand and pull from

4. Stay fresh
   - Outdated content becomes invisible
   - Update your best pages regularly

5. Track influence, not just rankings
   - Are you being cited in AI answers?
   - Citations are the new rankings

Key insight: Search hasn't disappeared — it's just moved one layer up. Winning there requires writing for AI extraction, not just human readers.

## Post 2
**Topic:** Google Is Actively Redefining What Traffic Means — And Content Marketers Need to Adapt

Google isn't accidentally letting traffic slip — it's strategically redefining what traffic means. With AI-generated answers satisfying user intent directly on the results page, Google is becoming the destination rather than the gateway.

Why Google is doing this:
- If it didn't evolve, AI-native competitors could bypass search entirely
- Google is choosing to compress the funnel now — owning more of the user journey
- Even if it means fewer clicks leaving its ecosystem

What this means for content marketers:
- Chasing rankings and raw clicks is no longer the north star
- Visibility is now layered — you might win by being cited in an AI summary or by shaping the narrative that gets synthesised
- A #1 ranking that doesn't get clicked may still be valuable
- A #3 result that used to drive traffic might now be invisible beneath an AI block

The question shifts from "How do I get clicks?" to "How do I become the source AI systems trust, cite, and summarise?"

What that requires:
- Depth over volume — thin SEO-first content is the first thing AI compresses away
- Authority over optimisation — original insights and data are harder for AI to replicate or ignore
- Brand over anonymity — if users don't need to click, they need to recognise you anyway

Key insight: Visibility is becoming less about occupying space and more about influencing outcomes. Success should be measured not just in sessions but in presence — are you shaping the answer, even when you're not the destination?

## Post 3
**Topic:** Reddit as a Keyword Research Tool — Finding Intent Your Competitors Are Missing

Most people do keyword research backwards — they open a tool, chase search volume, and start producing content. But Reddit is a live feed of search intent that competitors are overlooking.

4-step Reddit keyword research framework:

1. Start with what Google is already ranking
   - Search: site:reddit.com [your keyword]
   - Shows Reddit threads already performing in search
   - Pay attention to titles and highlighted snippets — that's the language Google connects to the query

2. Use subreddit data to uncover gaps
   - Drop a relevant subreddit into Ahrefs Site Explorer
   - Look at organic keywords ranking in positions 3-10
   - If Reddit is ranking but not dominating, that's your opening to create something more structured

3. Let real engagement guide you
   - Sort posts by "Top" and look at what's getting traction
   - High upvotes = proven interest
   - Pay attention to how people phrase things — "How do I…", "Why does…", "I finally figured out…"
   - This wording often outperforms anything from a keyword tool

4. Monitor brand and competitor mentions
   - Reddit threads increasingly get pulled into AI-generated answers
   - If people are discussing competitors (and not you) in "best X" threads, that's visibility you're missing
   - Tracking those conversations shows where demand already exists

Key insight: The real value of Reddit research isn't just finding keywords — it's understanding the intent behind them. That's the difference between content that just ranks and content that genuinely converts.
