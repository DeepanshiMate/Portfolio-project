## Post 1
**Topic:** 7-Step AI SEO Playbook for SaaS CMOs to Beat Giant Competitors in 90 Days

Based on data from 40+ active SaaS clients at Breaking B2B:

1. Build your money keyword and prompt matrix
   - Column 1: Every way someone refers to your software
   - Column 2: Every industry you actually sell into (check your CRM)
   - Column 3: Every competitor that comes up on sales calls
   - Column 4: Dream clients' jobs to be done and struggling moments

2. Make long-tail money keywords and prompts
   - Combine columns to build long-tail money keywords
   - These have low search volume but high buying intent
   - Example: "Best LinkedIn ads attribution software" or "HockeyStack alternative"
   - Competitor pages can rank within days

3. Run a visibility audit
   - Google each money keyword AND search it in an LLM
   - Check if your brand appears in ChatGPT, Claude, and other LLMs
   - Look at what pages are being cited — usually listicles and G2/Capterra/Reddit

4. Build a dedicated page for each money keyword
   - Match the intent of top results
   - Include comparison tables, pricing, product screenshots, customer reviews
   - Be honest about where you're a good and poor fit

5. Earn spots on external sites
   - Target sites being pulled into ChatGPT and ranking on Google
   - Request top 3 placements in roundups
   - Offer podcast features or interviews in exchange for placements

6. Use AI to draft, humans to make it real
   - AI can pull customer research and build outlines
   - Always add human touch — especially for high contract value solutions
   - Pages that don't resonate with dream clients will eventually tank

7. Stay focused and keep going
   - Work through every money keyword and prompt
   - Review each on Google and in AI search
   - Build best-in-class pages and earn backlinks and brand mentions

## Post 3
**Topic:** 7-Step Boring SaaS AI SEO Strategy That Prints Demos in 90 Days

1. Nail your blueprint
   - Map every way a prospect could search for your offering when in buying mode

2. Update and build an epic moat on your website
   - Use Ahrefs to find bottom funnel pages ranking but not #1
   - Prioritize these for updates, link building, and brand mentions
   - Fuel page copy with customer research, unique stats, product visuals, and proof points

3. Leverage pages to cook demos
   - Build your own listicle articles for competitor alternative terms
   - Position your software backed by customer research
   - Find external "best X software" articles and reach out to get featured

4. Win from past media
   - Find executives who have done press and podcasts
   - Reach out to sites that cited them without giving brand mentions or backlinks

5. Control the narrative
   - Google and LLMs love to cite Reddit — be helpful on relevant threads
   - Guest on top industry podcasts — ask hosts for brand mentions and backlinks
   - Prioritize YouTube channel and podcast if resources allow

6. Speed beats huge players
   - Large competitors have slow approval processes
   - Speed is your unfair advantage against slow-moving giants

7. It's an ecosystem
   - SEO/AEO works best as part of a wider channel ecosystem
   - SEO, LinkedIn, and YouTube together are currently driving demos for Breaking B2B clients
