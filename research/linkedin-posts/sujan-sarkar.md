## Post 1
**Topic:** How SEO Has Changed — Old SEO vs New SEO

SEO has changed more in the past 2 years than in the last 20. Key shifts every business needs to understand:

| Old SEO | New SEO |
|---------|---------|
| Google or nothing | AI search is here — must appear in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity |
| E-E-A-T optional | E-E-A-T mandatory — proof and lived experience required |
| Keywords ran the show | Intent runs the show |
| Volume over depth | Authority over volume — depth and interlinked clusters win |
| Write for crawlers | Write for AI engines — structured answers AI can cite |
| Any backlink will do | Backlinks that count — trusted sources, PR, podcasts |
| Ranking was the finish line | Discovery never stops — AI summaries keep visibility alive |

Key insight: Businesses that stay flexible and adapt to both Google and AI search will have the competitive advantage.

## Post 2
**Topic:** Case Study — 367.9M Visits and 1900% LLM Traffic Growth for AI SaaS Client

An AI SaaS client generated over 367.9M visits from June 2023 to May 2025, with 80% coming from search engines.

Key results:
- Monthly SEO traffic grew from 2.5M to 18.5M (76% growth)
- Monthly LLM traffic grew from 2K to 40K (1900% growth in one year)

What they did differently:
- Stayed focused on SEO fundamentals even during the AI explosion
- Optimized high-leverage pages to capture more traffic with less effort
- Strategically prepared for LLMs through Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- Built high-quality, niche-specific backlinks to boost authority

Key insight: SEO is not dead — it just needs to evolve. Businesses that adapted SEO for AI-driven traffic saw massive growth while others debated whether SEO was obsolete.
