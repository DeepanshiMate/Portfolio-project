## Post 2
**Topic:** Two Internets — Building for Humans AND for AI Machines

Most marketers are still optimizing for the wrong internet.

The internet humans see (surface layer):
- Pretty websites
- Sharp copy
- Tight design
- Storytelling that moves people

The internet AI reads (five levels deep):
- Where buying decisions are quietly being made right now
- When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best in your category?" your homepage doesn't show up
- Your $500K creative campaign doesn't impact AI responses
- Your $40K white paper with executive quotes isn't there either

Key insight: Build once for humans. Build deeper for machines. The question is whether you're building for both internets — or still designing for just one.

This concept reframes the entire challenge of AI-powered SEO content production. It's not about choosing between human-focused and AI-focused content — it's about building content that works at both levels simultaneously.

## Post 3
**Topic:** YouTube Ranked a New Video Above 3 Years of Blog Posts — in 24 Hours

YouTube is the most underinvested channel for B2B SaaS today.

Real result: Created 3 YouTube videos for a client. Within 24 hours, the videos ranked above content competitors had been trying to outrank with blog posts for 3 years.

The approach Foundation uses:
1. Prioritize low density keywords that have a high CPC in Google Ads
2. Identify a content creation strategy for these video assets
3. Create high quality YouTube videos following best practices
4. Distribute those YouTube videos with SEO and paid media coverage
5. Rinse and repeat

Key insight: You can capture traffic with a YouTube video for the same keywords you're already spending thousands on with paid ads — at a fraction of the cost.
