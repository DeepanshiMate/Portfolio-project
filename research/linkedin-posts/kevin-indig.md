## Post 1
**Topic:** Intent Compression — How AI Overviews Are Erasing Search Behavior Signals

For 20 years, what you searched told Google and SEOs how you'd behave:
- Type a brand name (navigational search) — in and out in seconds
- Search "best CRM for startups" (comparison search) — settle into comparison pages
- Intent sorted everyone into predictable behavior patterns

AI Overviews erased that signal. By dropping a block of answer text at the top of the page, it pulls every searcher into a reading session regardless of why they came.
- The brand-name searcher reads the AIO
- The product researcher reads another
- Both slow down, both stay, both behave alike on the SERP

Key insight: This "intent compression" means SEOs can no longer predict user behavior from search intent alone. Everyone is being treated the same by the SERP — which fundamentally changes how content needs to be structured and optimized.

## Post 2
**Topic:** SEO vs AEO — What's the Same and What's Actually Different

Saying AEO and SEO are the same misses the point. Search rank still drives a lot of AI visibility and most tactics overlap — but AEO is a different game.

What's the same between SEO and AEO:
1. Technical accessibility — machines need to crawl, render, parse, and understand content
2. Clear information architecture — pages, entities, topics, and relationships need to be easy to map
3. Content quality — thin, vague, generic content performs poorly in both systems
4. Authority signals — credible sources, expert content, links, mentions, and citations still matter
5. User intent matching — content needs to answer what people are actually trying to solve

What's new in AEO:
1. Rank matters after inclusion — in shortlists, first still wins; in narrative answers, mention quality matters
2. Mentions can rival links — reviews, comparisons, forums, articles, and docs can shape the answer
3. Prompts are harder to track — roughly 5x longer than keywords and harder to target page by page
4. Positioning becomes machine-interpreted — AI infers your category, competitors, use cases, and buyer fit from the broader web
5. Reputation gets compressed — owned content, third-party coverage, reviews, and consensus collapse into one answer

Key insight: AEO isn't a replacement for SEO — it's an additional layer that requires different thinking about how brands appear across AI search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

## Post 3
**Topic:** 846K Google Sessions Reveal How Users Behave Differently in AI Mode vs AI Overviews

Real data from a clickstream study of 846K US Google sessions from February and March 2026 — the largest public study of its kind.

The pattern: AI Mode is autoplay. AI Overviews is the Netflix browse.

AI Mode behavior:
- 88% of users take the AI's shortlist as-is
- 74% pick the top result
- 64% click nothing at all
- Completely closed loop

AI Overviews behavior:
- Cursor coverage hits 83% of the visible page (vs 66% without an AIO)
- Stillness rises from 29% to 44%
- Back-scrolling makes up nearly half of all scroll activity
- Users read, weigh, scroll past, then circle back to re-read

Key insight: AI Mode optimization is a visibility problem at the model layer. AI Overview optimization is a comparison problem on the SERP itself. These require completely different strategies — and treating them the same is a mistake most marketers are currently making.
