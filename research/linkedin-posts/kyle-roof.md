## Post 1
**Topic:** Content Silo Framework — How to Structure AI and Human SEO Content

Not all SEO content is the same. Every client needs a content silo made of:

- Top Level Content — The "parent" page for your main keyword (service, location, product)
- Supporting Content — Articles that strengthen the top level page's authority and rankings

Four ways to deliver this content:

1. Content Brief and Outline — A marked-up page outline, schema, and instructions for writers to execute
2. Existing Page Update Brief — A detailed brief to re-optimize published pages, includes schema and instructions
3. AI-Generated Draft — A full draft delivered fast using AI and POP optimization, team handles final proofing
4. Human-Edited Content — Fully written and fact-checked page, optimized for structure, accuracy, and SEO

Key insight: The right content format depends on your workflow — whether you have an in-house team or want everything handled externally. AI drafts are fastest but require human proofing before publishing.

## Post 2
**Topic:** Two Metrics That Actually Matter for Scaling Organic Growth

Most agencies track too many numbers — rankings, clicks, impressions, bounce rates, time on page. But when scaling organic growth, only two metrics really matter:

1. Content Velocity — How quickly can you go from keyword idea to published post?
   - If it takes weeks or months, competitors will outpace you
   - Speed compounds over time
   - Target: publishing in as little as 7 days from audit to publish

2. Content Alignment — Are you targeting keywords you can actually rank for right now?
   - Too many agencies chase competitive terms their site isn't ready for
   - Use tools like POP Impact Score to find the "sweet spot" of achievable keywords

Key insight: When you get both content velocity and content alignment right, rankings, traffic, and conversions follow naturally. These two metrics predict content machine health better than any vanity metric.
