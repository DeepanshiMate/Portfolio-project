## Post 2
**Topic:** Seasonal Recovery Playbook — 170% Organic Revenue Growth After Google Spam Update

A client was hit hard by Google's Spam Update. By March 2026, organic revenue grew 170.12% year over year and monthly sessions nearly doubled (1,968 to 3,868).

The 5-step recovery playbook:

1. Audit and rebuild after algorithm hit
   - Audit affected pages by commercial value
   - Rewrite weak content with experience-led detail
   - Disavow manipulative backlinks and rebuild through editorial links

2. Hammer your USP into product pages
   - Rewrite copy to target customization-intent queries
   - Add structured FAQs, schema markup, and benefit-led headings
   - Restructure internal links so authority flows from blogs to commercial pages

3. Time everything around your seasonal peak
   - Publish all updates 6-10 weeks before demand peak
   - Use year-over-year data, not month-over-month
   - Refresh key landing pages before peak, not during it

4. Fix your Google Merchant Center feed
   - Audit for missing attributes and incorrect product types
   - Rewrite product titles with primary keywords and key specifications
   - Populate every required attribute

5. Replace ad-hoc blogging with topical clusters
   - Research long-tail queries and build content calendars around topic clusters
   - Link every article to relevant product and category pages
   - Use clear headings and FAQ schema for AI search visibility

Results after 5 months:
- Organic sessions: 1,968 to 3,868 (+96.54% YoY)
- Organic revenue: +170.12% YoY
