## Post 1
**Topic:** How to Elevate AI Content for SEO and AEO

Both SEO and AEO work when you have the right ingredients. Key steps to elevate AI content:

1. Build your search brainiac
   - Data layer: GSC, GA4, AI search and rank tracking
   - Company layer: products, services, FAQs
   - Expertise layer: founder interviews, subject matter expert input, user generated content

2. Create a swipe file
   - Study top competitors
   - Convert their best content into templates
   - Repeat for around 100 assets

3. Research the right way
   - Identify top competitors for your target keyword from both traditional search and AI citations
   - Extract entities and content structure

4. Perform a page-level gap analysis
   - Use ChatGPT to identify topic gaps among top competitors

5. Build your purple cow content strategy
   - Combine what works, what's relevant, and the gaps to create a unique content angle

6. Create a killer content brief including:
   - Intelligence from your search brainiac
   - At least 3 proven templates matching the content type
   - Most important entities to cover
   - Purple cow differentiation

## Post 2
**Topic:** Effort is the Real Differentiator in AI Search

Most SERPs and AI citation sets are polluted with AI-generated content with no differentiation. Just 10% more effort separates you from most competitors.

Real example: The keyword "best SEO books" — ranked for years without updating the content, even after moving it across two different domains. It still dominates both traditional SERPs and AI citations.

Why? He purchased every single SEO book, read all of them, and wrote genuine reviews. The differentiator was real effort and real experience.

Key insight: Everyone is chasing automation and minimum effort. The pendulum is swinging back — putting in more effort will help you stand out in a sea of low-effort AI content.

## Post 3
**Topic:** Finding Unknown Keywords Before SEO Tools Catch Up

Most competitors chase search volume using old data. The winning strategy is finding trending topics before SEO tools catch up.

Best sources for finding unknown keywords:
- TikTok
- X (Twitter)
- Quora
- Reddit
- Exploding Topics
- Niche forums (search "niche + forum" in Google)

Being an early mover on emerging topics means winning without much competition.

He also shared an agent prompt that identifies 15-30 emerging and accelerating topics within a specific industry, then translates them into actionable content opportunities with trend scoring, topical clustering, and prioritized execution plans.
