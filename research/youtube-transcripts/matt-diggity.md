## Key Insights from Transcript

### 1. Editorial Media Makes Up 61% of AI Generated Responses
Research by Profound shows that editorial media makes up 61% of AI generated responses — your own website accounts for only 44%.

Real example: A simple query about a major Google product launch didn't cite any of Google's own content. Instead it led with a Washington Post article review. Google isn't even citing Google for stories about Google.

Key insight: If you're not earning good press coverage you're almost invisible to AI search — regardless of how strong your on-site SEO is.

### 2. Digital PR is the Backdoor Into LLM Citations
AI search tools don't just pull information from your website — they pull from sources all across the internet to build a picture of your business. If your website is featured in high authority editorial sites, LLMs are essentially forced to place you in their answers.

Real client results:
- 313 press placements including Google News, Yahoo, and AP News
- Over 1,000 visits from AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini
- Monthly revenue jumped from £2,000 to £17,400 per month
- Organic traffic more than doubled

### 3. Five Step Digital PR Framework

**Step 1 — Find the Right Coverage Sites**
- Search your core topics in Google and note which key publications keep showing up
- Those outlets already hold topical authority and are the ones AI tools trust
- Use Ahrefs Content Explorer — filter by referring domains sorted by highest
- Ask LLM tools directly who their most trusted writers or sites are in your niche
- Prioritize authority over size — one link from BBC, Reuters, or Wall Street Journal (DA 90+) does more than dozens of links from lower tier sites

**Step 2 — Build Your Story**
Content formats that consistently get picked up:
- Data studies and original research — LLMs actively prioritize cited data over evidence-free opinions
- Newsjacking — spot a breaking story, find a killer angle, and secure a high DR backlink fast
- Industry surveys — platforms like Attest can run targeted surveys (note: costs can add up)

**Step 3 — Write the Press Release**
Structure that works:
- Headline — anyone should understand the story and why they should care just from the headline
- Lead — flesh out who, what, when, where, and why with credibility established in two lines
- Key facts — the data, findings, and specifics that back up the lead
- Quote — a confident statement from your company showing authority
- CTA and company bio

**Step 4 — Pitch Smart**
- Use tools like Muckrack and Hunter.io to find writers and editors contact details
- Personalize every pitch — explain specifically why the story matters for their readers
- Keep pitches 150-200 words maximum
- Subject line is the most important part — be clear but don't give everything away
- Example: "Investment Platform Grows Revenue 750% Without a Single Paid Ad" — data, intrigue, and a result
- Follow up once after 3-5 days with a new angle or data point, then move on

**Step 5 — Measure What's Meaningful**
- Focus on revenue and conversions, not just audience reach
- Track PR links and organic traffic through high authority backlinks
- Track AI citations using tools like Otterly.ai across multiple AI platforms
- Track unique referring domains in Ahrefs Site Explorer under Referring Domains report
