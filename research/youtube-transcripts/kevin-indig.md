## Key Insights from Transcript

### 1. 2024 Was Peak Traffic Year — Clicks Are Declining, Impressions Rising
Kevin's view: 2024 was the peak traffic year. Traffic as a whole — not just organic — will likely never be higher. Clicks are going down while impressions are going up, largely because every URL cited in an AI Overview automatically gets an impression once it loads.

Key data point: Over 85% of people click the "load more" button on AI Overviews because they want to see the full answer. However most people only read the upper third of the AI Overview — making early citations the most valuable.

### 2. Traffic Loss is NOT Directly Correlated With Revenue Loss
Across multiple clients, Kevin is seeing revenue relatively flat despite significant traffic declines. The correlation between traffic loss and revenue loss is low for most business types.

Who is struggling:
- Publishers heavily reliant on AdSense
- Affiliate SEO sites

Who is flat to up:
- SaaS companies
- E-commerce (limited impact so far)

Key insight: Some SaaS companies are actually seeing higher revenue after the launch of AI Overviews because people get better answers faster and reach conversion points much more quickly. AI traffic converts at a much higher rate than organic traffic — it's just still very small in volume.

### 3. Google vs ChatGPT — Different Winners in Different Markets
In consolidated markets (like CRM software dominated by Salesforce and HubSpot), the same winners on Google are also winners on ChatGPT.

In fragmented markets, the same winners on Google are NOT the same winners on ChatGPT. Reasons include:
- Some sites accidentally block LLMs
- Old school SEO approaches that don't translate to LLM visibility
- Different content formats and sources being prioritized

Critical recommendation: At minimum, every company should verify that they dominate their market on ChatGPT the same way they do on Google — because it's not always true.

### 4. External Validation is More Important Than Ever
People generally like and believe AI Overviews — but they also like to validate AI answers on platforms like Reddit, YouTube, and forums. People use these in tandem with Google search as a verification check.

Key sources people use to validate AI answers:
- Reddit
- YouTube
- Trustpilot and G2
- Google Maps and review platforms

Implication: It's more important than ever to influence what other platforms are saying about your brand — not just your own website. What other websites say about you has become potentially more important than what you say about yourself.

### 5. Different LLMs Pull From Different Sources
Each LLM has different biases in where it pulls citations from:
- Google and Gemini are biased towards Reddit
- Perplexity leans heavily towards Wikipedia
- ChatGPT uses Bing for grounding

SEO has become multi-platform and multi-channel — you need to be visible and well-represented on the specific platforms each LLM trusts most.

### 6. How to Grow Coverage in AI Overviews
Three main ingredients:
1. Content freshness — how recently was your content updated?
2. Content relevance — how closely does your content match what people are actually prompting?
3. Brand authority and trust — have people heard of you? What sentiment do they associate with your brand?

The more specific the prompt being targeted, the easier it is to understand what a good answer looks like and create content that appears in AI Overviews.

### 7. Two Powerful AI Use Cases for SEO Content Production

**Use Case 1 — Analyze Sales Calls at Scale**
Kevin's work with Ramp ($16B fintech company): Used AI to analyze transcripts from 50+ sales conversations between prospects who became customers. Asked questions like: What problems are they facing? What solutions are they using now? What does success look like?

Result: Created blog articles that converted at a much higher rate because they were grounded in real customer language and real buying triggers — not search volume data.

Key insight: Almost nobody in SEO actually listens to customer conversations. AI now makes it possible to analyze hundreds of conversations at scale to identify what topics people genuinely care about.

**Use Case 2 — Build Interactive Tools and Experiences**
Using vibe coding tools like V0, Lovable, and Cursor to create calculators, quizzes, generators, and widgets that make content more interactive.

Benefits:
- Higher engagement with content
- Better conversion rates
- Can be embedded across multiple pages
- Allows marketers to create proof-of-concept tools without a developer

Caveat: Developers are still significantly more efficient than non-developers at vibe coding. Having a dedicated developer on the marketing team who can vibe code quickly is the ideal setup.

### 8. SEO is Not Dead — It's in a New Cycle
Kevin's view: SEO has "died" many times and revitalized each time. The current disruption is real but the first principles of SEO still apply — content freshness, relevance, and brand authority matter just as much if not more.

The biggest difference between AI search optimization and classic SEO is not what SEOs do — it's what users are doing. Users are now searching with much longer, more specific prompts because AI search handles long-tail intent so much better than classic Google ever did.

This creates a vacuum of hyper-specific content that almost nobody is creating yet — a major opportunity for brands that act now.
