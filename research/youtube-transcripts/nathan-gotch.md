## Key Insights from Transcript

### 1. Newsjacking — The Fastest Way to Rank in Google AND AI Search
When HubSpot launched a new AEO tracking tool, Nathan's team immediately built two dedicated assets:
- HubSpot AEO alternatives
- Rankability versus HubSpot AEO

Result: Within 6 hours, one page ranked #1 on Google AND was being cited inside Google's AI Overviews. Rankability was being recommended as an alternative directly in the AI-generated answer.

Key insight: SEO results don't always take 30-90 days. When dealing with emerging keywords or brand new trends, that timeline can compress dramatically if you move fast and know what you're doing.

### 2. First Mover Advantage in AI SEO
When a new topic appears there is little to no competition. If you can identify the opportunity quickly and publish as fast as possible, you have a chance to be the first strong result in that market.

Important caveat: This works much better if your website already has authority, trust, and an established link profile. New websites will find this much harder.

### 3. Build Clusters, Not Single Assets
Don't publish one random asset and hope for the best. Build a group of assets around the topic attacking the query from different angles. This matters especially because of query fanout.

Query fanout strategy: Predict what the AI is likely to fan out and search for behind the scenes, then build assets that map to those possible subqueries. Nathan's team was cited twice in Google's AI Mode retrieval sources because they built two assets aligned with likely fanout queries.

### 4. Content Structure That Works for AI — "AI Bait"
For listicle style content:
- Frontload the actual list as high in the content as possible — don't make users scroll
- Always include a comparison table — Nathan calls this "AI bait" because AI systems love tables and often pull directly from them to use in AI-generated answers

### 5. Parasite SEO for New Websites
If you don't have an authoritative website yet, publish on platforms that already have authority:
- LinkedIn
- Medium
- YouTube (if you have an established channel)

This gives you initial lift while your own domain builds authority.

### 6. When Data Isn't Enough — Use Strategic Judgment
When targeting new keywords, there may not be enough competitor data to rely on traditional content optimization tools. Example: When Rankability first launched in 2024, the term was a scientific term — using a content optimization tool would have pushed content in a completely irrelevant direction.

Key lesson: When dealing with new keywords without useful competitor intelligence, rely more on strategic judgment than on data alone.

### 7. The Big Takeaway
Stay in tune with your industry. Always look for emerging trends, product launches, new features, and new conversations. When you spot something promising — move immediately. Early ranking signals compound — the more signals you collect early, the stronger your ranking moat becomes.
