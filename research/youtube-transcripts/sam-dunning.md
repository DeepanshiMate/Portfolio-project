## Key Insights from Transcript

### 1. Google Confirms — AEO and GEO Are Still Just SEO
Google dropped their official guide on AI search optimization on May 15, 2026. Their key statement: "Optimizing for generative AI search is optimizing for the search experience — thus still SEO."

The best practices from traditional SEO continue to be fully relevant in AI features. Google search is rooted in their core search ranking quality systems, and AI techniques highlight content from the search index through RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation), also known as grounding.

Key implication: Many loud voices claiming AEO and GEO are brand new playbooks are being directly contradicted by Google's own official guidance.

### 2. How RAG and Query Fanout Actually Work
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) — when you search for something specific at the bottom of the funnel (best X software, competitor alternatives, specific use cases), the AI tool can't pull from its own historic data. It does an active search within Google.

Important: ChatGPT is also pulling from Google search when it does grounding — not just Google's own AI tools.

Query fanout — models request more information to fetch additional relevant search results. Example: a top funnel search "how to fix a garden full of weeds" fans out into queries like "best herbicides for lawns", "remove weeds without chemicals", "how to prevent weeds in lawns."

### 3. What Google Says You Actually Need to Do
- Create valuable non-commodity content — pull expertise from sales calls, customer success calls, and support calls
- Provide a unique point of view — customer research is critical for understanding dream clients' problems, goals, and motivators
- Use E-E-A-T signals — experience, expertise, authority, and trust
- Organize content with bottom line up front — quick summary, comparison table, and why you should trust us at the top
- High quality visuals and video — YouTube videos embedded on relevant pages can be pulled from in LLMs
- Focus on what users want — avoid rambling to hit word counts, get to the point quickly

### 4. What Google Says You Do NOT Need to Do
- LLMs.txt files or other special markup — not necessary
- Chunking content specifically for AI — AI tools decide how to chunk, not you
- Rewriting content just for AI systems — write for humans first
- Seeking inauthentic mentions — spamming Reddit or irrelevant sites with your brand

### 5. The Danger of Mass AI Content Generation
Several SaaS companies have been hit hard for generating thousands of articles or landing pages with AI at the click of a button. What typically happens:
- Quick win — surge in organic traffic initially
- Google detects low value, spammy content
- Traffic suddenly tanks — sometimes entire websites get penalized

Sam's recommendation: Use AI to pull from customer research, sales calls, and keyword data. Use AI for outlines and first drafts. But always have a human in the loop to make sure the content resonates with dream clients.

### 6. Practical Three Step Process for SaaS AI SEO
1. Customer research first — know your dream clients' goals, frustrations, jobs to be done, and why they choose you. Use MCPs from tools like Fathom, Fireflies, or Gong to pull sales call insights directly into Claude.

2. Map money keywords and prompts — what your dream clients search for when actively evaluating software. Competitor alternatives, competitor vs competitor comparisons, specific use cases, integrations, and pricing pages.

3. Build and optimize content — refresh existing pages lagging on page two of Google or not being cited in AI search. Build new dedicated pages for each money keyword. Then do off-page work — backlinks, brand mentions, and getting featured in relevant review sites and roundup listicles.

### 7. Brand Mentions in AI Search — The Right Way
Brand mentions are hugely important for AI search visibility. But doing it the wrong way (spamming Reddit or irrelevant sites) can get you banned.

The right approach:
- Manually identify niche relevant sites ranking well in Google organic and being cited in AI search for your money keywords
- Reach out with a personalized Loom video or LinkedIn message
- Offer a value exchange — feature them on your podcast, add them to your listicle, or some fair trade
- Some will be pay-to-play — sometimes a necessary investment
