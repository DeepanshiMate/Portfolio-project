## Key Insights from Transcript

### 1. AI Mode Adoption is Still Small But Growing Fast
Google AI Mode currently represents around 1-2% of all US search — similar to news and shopping tabs. However both Aleyda and Chris expect this to increase significantly as Google finds ways to monetize AI Mode. The growth has already peaked and flattened in early data, but long-term trajectory is upward.

### 2. SEO vs GEO/AEO — Similar Concepts, Different Execution
The core optimization areas between traditional SEO and AI search optimization are very similar. The biggest differences are:
- How results are tracked and reported — old tracking paradigms feel "very dated very quickly"
- The level of personalization in AI results vs traditional search
- Query fan-out technique — AI search runs many questions in parallel, not just one
- Results presentation and delivery is fundamentally different

Chris Green's take: "This is probably one of the most significant changes I've ever seen in SEO."

### 3. Ranking vs Visibility — A Critical Mindset Shift
The ranking paradigm is no longer useful as a primary metric. Key insight: "An arbitrary keyword search at a given point repeated daily or weekly is no longer a great proxy for how well you perform."

You have to be visible in the contexts that are valuable to you — not just rank for keywords.

### 4. Think in Passages, Not Pages
Google is now "scrapbooking" — pulling snippets from different sources and assembling them into answers. This means:
- A single page is no longer enough for any given user intent
- SEOs need to stop being preoccupied with URLs and information architecture across URLs
- Content needs to be structured in retrievable passages, not just full pages

### 5. Originality is the Missing Piece
With AI generating content at scale, originality is becoming the key differentiator. If AI has all the world's data, how do you signal that you have the original take and aren't just a summary of a summary?

Chris Green: "We're still at risk of saying query fan-out means lots of FAQs everywhere in short chunks — and I think we're still missing the originality piece."

### 6. New KPIs for the Generative AI Search Era
Traditional metrics need to be refreshed. New metrics to consider:
- Chunk retrieval frequency
- AI citation count
- Vector index presence
- Retrieval confidence score
- LLM answer coverage
- AI mode call success rate
- Semantic density score

Key recommendation: Focus on 3-4 of these alongside conversion and revenue — not all of them at once.

### 7. Quality Over Volume — The HubSpot Lesson
HubSpot's approach to content in the AI era focuses on:
- Thinking about the customer first, not popularity metrics
- Staying on topic and on brand with business expertise
- Content that drives business goals AND helps the audience
- Even zero-click content has value if it communicates expertise and authority
